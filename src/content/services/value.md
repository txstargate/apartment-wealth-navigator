The free value range is the quick version. This page is about the deeper work.

Value Navigator, the service, is a broker opinion of value (BOV), an evaluation of an apartment building in Washington DC, Virginia, or Maryland, where I am licensed. It is a deeper analysis than the free range, and it can be prepared for a lender such as a bank.

## A broker opinion of value is not an appraisal

A BOV is an evaluation, not a formal valuation. It is not an appraisal, and it does not replace a licensed appraisal where a lender or the law requires one. If a lender asks for an appraisal, you need an appraisal. Ask the lender which one it needs first.

## Start with the free value range

The [free Value Navigator range](/value-navigator/) is the first step. Send the rent roll and the trailing 12 months, and I return a conservative, base, and optimistic value with the assumptions written out. It shows where your numbers stand.

## Who does what

I am a real estate advisor and broker. I am not an appraiser, an attorney, or a tax advisor, and this page is not appraisal, legal, tax, or lending advice. Rules differ by city, county, and state. Consult a licensed professional where the property is located.
